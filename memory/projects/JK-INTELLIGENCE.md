# JerryKnows Intelligence

**Category:** AI

## Overview
A proposed JerryKnows intelligence dashboard / desktop tool inspired by Palantir-style workflows and "Eye of God" concepts, built from public data sources.

## Current goal
Research a YouTube channel focused on geospatial intelligence / OSINT workflows and extract the top 5 critical capabilities for an MVP this week.

## Target product direction
- JerryKnows Intelligence dashboard
- task-driven tracking and monitoring
- publicly available data only
- Sentinel-1 satellite resources as a key source family
- geospatial tracking, monitoring, and alerting
- news feed tracking for on-the-ground situations
- public US Government and military memos, warnings, travel information, and status feeds
- air traffic, rail traffic, and other infrastructure monitoring where publicly available

## Desired MVP shape
A user can assign a task, and JK tracks the task across relevant public feeds and updates a dashboard.

## Product direction update
- Jon wants this served over the internet and specifically integrated into `jongrayson.com`.
- The preferred architecture direction is: **JerryKnows as the backend/data engine**, and **jongrayson.com as the public-facing intelligence experience**.
- The product should feel unmistakably next-gen, with a globe-first interface rather than a plain admin dashboard.
- The goal is a powerful **public-source intelligence / geospatial analysis platform**. Keep it focused on lawful OSINT, situational awareness, infrastructure monitoring, and analyst workflows.
- Important terminology note: in this project context, **"targeter" means an intelligence analyst focused on a specific subject/target set**, not anything violent or malicious.

## Planned research outputs
- top 5 capabilities from channel transcripts

- MVP architecture for this week
- reusable watcher pattern for intelligence feeds

## Early top-5 MVP capabilities
1. **4D geospatial timeline view** that fuses map + time + event playback for a task/incident.
2. **Multi-source traffic and infrastructure monitoring** for AIS, ADS-B, chokepoints, ports, and other critical logistics.
3. **Sentinel-1 / SAR change detection layer** for infrastructure, motion, and weather/night-robust monitoring.
4. **Task-based watcher agents** where a user assigns a monitoring objective and JK keeps tracking it across sources.
5. **Situation feed / alert stack** that combines news, government advisories, military/public warnings, travel status, and field signals into one dashboard.

## Additional dashboard idea backlog from Bilawal channel scan
- chokepoint monitoring modules (ports, straits, pipelines, air corridors)
- "dark activity" detection for vessels/traffic that disappear, reroute, or behave abnormally
- satellite overpass planner / pass timeline layer for showing what sensors were overhead and when
- RF / signals intelligence-inspired layer later, using lawful public-source emitter or comms-adjacent data where available
- 3D scene / environment reconstruction as a future premium layer, not week-1 MVP
- route disruption tracking for aviation, maritime, and supply chain flows
- anomaly cards that explain what changed and why it matters, not just raw detections
- auto-generated JK briefings that summarize the operational picture by region/task

## Important product note
- "Real-time" for the MVP should mean **near-real-time fusion** from public feeds (AIS, ADS-B, advisories, news, social, weather, etc.), with Sentinel-1/SAR acting as a powerful confirmation/change-detection layer rather than truly continuous live video.
