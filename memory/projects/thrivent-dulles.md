## The Challenge
Financial services (Thrivent/FINRA/SEC) have incredibly strict compliance, archiving, and data privacy (NPI - Non-Public Personal Information) regulations. Standard AI tools (like public ChatGPT or Claude) cannot be used with client data due to privacy leaks and compliance risks.

## The Solution: Privacy-First AI
Implementing "Zero-Trust" or Local AI solutions where no client data ever leaves the office's control. A Mac Studio or dedicated secure on-premise server running local models can automate tedious workflows while remaining 100% compliant.

## Discovery Interview Guide (For Jon -> Marty)
To build the perfect automation pipeline, Jon needs to interview Marty. Here are the core areas to investigate:

### 1. Client Meeting Lifecycle
- How much time is spent prepping for a client review meeting? What documents are gathered?
- During the meeting, how are notes taken? (Pen/paper, typed, or recorded?)
- After the meeting, who drafts the follow-up email/letter? How long does that take?

### 2. Operational Bottlenecks (Ask Amy)
- When a client brings in outside statements (401k, bank accounts), who enters that data into your planning software?
- Which Thrivent/compliance forms take the longest to fill out?
- What is the most annoying repetitive task Amy has to do every week?

### 3. Client Communications & Marketing
- How does the office handle generic client touchpoints? (Birthdays, market updates, Thrivent Action Teams)
- Do they do seminars or workshops? If so, who handles the presentation drafting and follow-up sequences?

### 4. Software Stack
- What CRM is the office using? (Salesforce?)
- What financial planning software? (eMoney, MoneyGuidePro?)
- Are there any APIs or export capabilities we can tap into?
