# WOOF

**Category:** AI

## What it is
Woof is the working project/brand for Woof Bakery social and marketing creative.

## Current direction
- Brand: **Woof Bakery**
- Product focus: **dog treats**
- Desired creative style: a cross between **bold ad creative** and **meme/social-native**
- Recommended campaign vibe: **premium but funny**, bright, punchy, and scroll-stopping

## Core campaign theme
- **The Barkery Drop**

## Strong tagline options
- Treats Worth Sitting For
- Fresh Bakes for Good Dogs
- The Barkery Drop
- Spoil Them Properly
- Bakery Energy. Dog Approved.

## Creative guidance
- Lean into luxury dessert-ad energy, but for dogs
- Keep the tone cute, premium, and slightly absurd
- Prioritize product hero shots, dog reaction creatives, and bold text-led promos
- Best-fit image models discussed: Ideogram for text-heavy graphics, Recraft for clean branded visuals, Flux for strong visual vibe and dog/product imagery
