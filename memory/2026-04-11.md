# 2026-04-11

- Legal platform architecture for JerryKnows is now explicitly split across codebases by intent: `jerryknows-ai` is the legal/chat product base, while existing `jerryknows` / VPS `~/jerryknows-app` continues to host deployed legal search and DB tooling continuity.
- Core legal platform sequence is settled: Legal DB first, then retrieval/search, then watcher agents/cron updates, then deeper product features.
- Database direction is settled: use PostgreSQL as the primary legal DB, with Postgres full-text search first and pgvector later if semantic retrieval is needed.
- Contract review endpoints in `jerryknows-ai` were wired to query the internal legal DB before analysis. `/api/legal/review`, `/api/legal/policy-review`, and `/api/legal/scan-risk` now inject retrieved authority context and return authority `sources` when available.
- The live VPS `jk-ai` app was updated and restarted to match local contract-review retrieval changes.
- Legal watcher scheduling is live on the VPS via cron. IRS/Treasury and Virginia watcher jobs now run every 6 hours and log to `~/jerryknows-app/logs/legal-watchers.log`.
- Citation normalization for legal ingests was improved so source labels are cleaner and more structured (for example Virginia Register, Treasury press releases, IRS topics).
- Deeper core authority ingestion has started beyond news/register items. The legal DB now includes at least: `26 U.S.C. § 61`, `26 U.S.C. Chapter 61`, `Va. Code § 60.2-212`, and `Va. Code § 60.2-212.2`.
- The JerryKnows UI now distinguishes legal authorities from generic web-search sources with richer legal source cards rather than only plain source chips.
- A prompt-quality issue was discovered during testing: the model could cite plausible outside legal authorities not actually retrieved from the internal DB. Prompts were tightened so contract reviews explicitly cite only authorities present in the internal legal database block, and otherwise state that no specific internal authority matched.
