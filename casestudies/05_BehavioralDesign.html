Behavioral design, honestly

Driving adoption without dark patterns — by clearing the path to what people already want to do.

The problem

People want to adopt good tools. They usually don't for one of three reasons: they don't see the value, it's harder than what they do now, or nothing reminds them to try.

The model I used

Motivation

A 90-second demo showed the time saved.

+

Ability

Three core actions instead of twelve.

+

Prompt

A nudge at the exact right moment.

=
Adoptionall three, lined up

B.J. Fogg's model: a behavior happens only when motivation, ability, and a well-timed prompt meet. Most things fail because one is missing — not because the feature is bad.
